
A LITTLE UNIVERSE FOR NAMOUSSTI ❤️

HOW TO PLAY LOCALLY
1. Keep index.html and the images folder together.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.
3. Internet is required for the Spotify player.

IMAGE FILES
images/couple.png
images/purple-memory.png

Keep the exact filenames and folder structure.
The photos are loaded locally by the website.

PUBLISH ONLINE FOR FREE
1. Open https://app.netlify.com/drop
2. Sign in or create a free account if prompted.
3. Drag the entire A-Little-Universe-for-Namoussti folder
   into the upload area, if the page accepts the folder.
   Otherwise, upload a ZIP of the folder.
4. Open the website URL Netlify gives you and test it.

IMPORTANT
Test the published site before sending it to Namoussti.
Make sure both photos load and the Spotify player appears.
Spotify may require her to press Play and may require a Spotify account.
